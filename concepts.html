🕵️ WH Question Words

Use question words to ask for a person, place, time, reason, thing, choice or manner.

WHO → person
Who called?
WHERE → place
Where are we going?
WHEN → time
When does it start?
WHY → reason
Why are you smiling?
WHAT → thing/info
What happened?
HOW → manner
How did you do it?

🧩 Build a Question

Choose the correctly ordered question.

Where + are + you + going?

Why + did + he + leave?

When + will + they + arrive?
